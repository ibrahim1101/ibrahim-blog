# University of Copenhagen Data Breach Exposes Data of 525,000 People

The University of Copenhagen confirmed a data breach that exposed the personal information of 525,000 individuals, including students, staff, and possibly others associated with the institution. The breach was detected on October 20, 2026, and officially disclosed on October 23, 2026. An update on October 25, 2026, revealed that an additional 20,000 individuals had their PIN codes exposed. The incident has raised concerns about the security of personal data stored in test environments and the potential for misuse of the exposed information.

## Confirmed Facts and Significance

The breach occurred in a **test environment**, which the university described as being used for development or testing purposes rather than for storing live or sensitive data. Despite this, the test environment contained a significant amount of personal data, including:

- Full names  
- National identity numbers (CPR)  
- Phone numbers  
- Email addresses  

Additionally, 20,000 individuals had their **PIN codes** exposed in a later update. The university has confirmed that it has **contained the attack** and **blocked the attacker’s access**, and there is **no indication** that systems supporting **daily operations** were accessed or disrupted.

The university has **reported the incident** to the **Danish Data Protection Agency** and stated that it falls under the **General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR)**. This regulatory framework imposes strict requirements on how institutions must handle personal data and report breaches, including notifying affected individuals and relevant authorities within a specific timeframe.

## Uncertainties and Missing Information

Despite the university’s disclosure, several aspects of the breach remain **uncertain** or **unconfirmed**. The university has stated that it is still **investigating the extent of the incident**, and the **exact data fields** that were exposed are **not fully confirmed**. Additionally, the **timeline of events**—including the detection, disclosure, and updates—has not been **independently verified**.

The university also claims that **daily operations were not disrupted**, but this assertion has not been independently confirmed. Furthermore, the **results of the internal investigation** have not yet been **fully released**, and **no further updates** have been independently confirmed.

## University Background

The University of Copenhagen is **Denmark’s oldest university**, founded in **1479**. It has approximately **36,800 students** and **10,700 employees**, with **six faculties** and **more than 200 research centers**. As a leading research institution, the university handles a vast amount of personal and sensitive data, making the breach a significant event in the context of data security and privacy.

## Impact and Recommendations

The exposure of personal data increases the **risk of phishing and social engineering attacks**. The university has advised affected individuals to be **cautious of unsolicited communications** and to **independently verify requests**. It has also **restricted physical access** to certain critical locations on campus as a precaution and is **gradually reopening** these areas as it distributes new access codes.
